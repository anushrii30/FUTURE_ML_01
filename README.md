# FUTURE_ML_01

## 📊 Business Forecast Insights

Based on the forecasting model results, sales are expected to show a gradual upward trend over the next 6 months.

### 🔍 Key Observations
- Sales demonstrate seasonal fluctuations with identifiable peak months.
- The overall trend indicates steady growth.
- Forecast values closely follow historical patterns.## 📌 Project Overview
This project develops a Machine Learning model to forecast future sales using historical business data.  

Accurate sales forecasting enables businesses to optimize inventory, allocate resources efficiently, and improve financial decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
Reliable demand forecasting helps organizations to:

- Prevent overstocking and stockouts  
- Reduce inventory holding costs  
- Improve cash flow management  
- Plan marketing and operational strategies effectively  

The goal of this project is to predict monthly sales trends to support data-driven business planning.

---

---

## ⚙️ Methodology

1. Data cleaning and date formatting  
2. Monthly sales aggregation  
3. Time-based feature engineering (Year, Month, Quarter)  
4. Creation of lag features  
5. Training a Linear Regression model  
6. Model evaluation using MAE and RMSE  
7. Forecasting sales for the next six months  

---

- The model captures seasonal sales patterns effectively.  
- Forecasted sales reflect realistic growth trends.  
- Predictions provide actionable insights for business planning.
